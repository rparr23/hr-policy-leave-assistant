# Contoso Business Travel Policy (demo excerpt)

Version 2026.1, effective 1 January 2026. Sample content for the HR Policy & Leave Assistant demo.

## §2 Approval
- All business travel needs approval before booking. Start the request through the HR assistant in Teams.
- Your manager approves every trip.
- Trips estimated above $2,500 also need Finance approval.
- International trips also need approval from the VP of Operations.

## §3 Air travel
- Book flights at least 14 days before departure. Shorter notice needs a written business justification.
- Economy class is standard. Business class is allowed only when a single flight leg is 6 hours or longer.

## §4 Per diem (meals and incidentals, per day including travel days)
| City | Per diem (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| New York | 92 |
| San Francisco | 90 |
| Chicago | 85 |
| London | 110 |
| All other cities | 75 |

## §5 Expenses
Submit receipts within 30 days of return. Alcohol, personal entertainment and upgrades paid for with company funds are not reimbursable.
