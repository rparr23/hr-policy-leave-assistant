## §7 Time Off

### §7.1 Annual leave (PTO)
- Full-time employees accrue 15 days a year (120 hours), credited each pay period.
- Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to scheduled hours.
- Up to 40 hours carry over into the next calendar year; the rest expires on 31 December unless state law requires payout.
- Request PTO through the HR assistant in Teams at least 5 working days ahead when possible. Your manager approves.
- A leave day for full-time employees is 8 hours; for part-time employees it is 4 hours.

### §7.2 Company holidays
New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day (observed 3 July 2026), Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and the day after, and Christmas Day. Holidays are not deducted from leave.

### §7.3 Sick leave
- Full-time employees receive 6 sick days (48 hours) a year.
- Part-time employees receive sick leave as required by the law of their work state.
- Where state or local law is more generous than this policy, the law applies.
- Sick leave may be used for your own illness, medical appointments, or to care for a family member.
- A medical certificate is required for absences of more than 3 consecutive working days.

### §7.4 Bereavement leave
Up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member (spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or in-law).

### §7.5 Jury duty
Employees summoned for jury duty receive full pay for up to 10 working days. Provide a copy of the summons.

### §7.6 Family and medical leave
- Contoso follows the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) and applicable state family and medical leave programs.
- Parental leave: 8 weeks of paid parental leave for birth, adoption or foster placement, for employees with 6+ months of service, running together with FMLA and state leave where eligible.
- All family, medical and parental leave is arranged with HR, not requested directly from your manager.

### §7.7 Unpaid leave
Up to 10 unpaid days a year with manager approval once PTO is used.

## §8 Workplace conduct
Contoso does not tolerate harassment, discrimination or retaliation. Report concerns to HR (hr@contoso.com), your HR partner, or the anonymous Ethics Line. Reports are handled confidentially.

## §9 Accommodations
Employees who need a workplace accommodation for a disability, pregnancy or religious practice should contact HR. HR works with you directly and confidentially.
