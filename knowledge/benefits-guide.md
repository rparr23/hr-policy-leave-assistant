# Contoso Benefits Guide (demo excerpt)

Plan year 2027. Sample content for the HR Policy & Leave Assistant demo.

## §1 Eligibility
Employees scheduled for 30+ hours a week are eligible for medical, dental and vision coverage from their first day. Part-time employees under 30 hours are eligible for the 401(k) and the Employee Assistance Program.

## §2 Open enrollment
Open enrollment for the 2027 plan year runs 2 to 16 November 2026. New hires must enroll within 30 days of their start date. Outside these windows, changes are allowed only after a qualifying life event (marriage, birth or adoption, loss of other coverage), reported within 30 days.

## §3 Medical plans
- PPO: $500 individual deductible, Contoso pays 85% of the premium.
- HDHP with HSA: $1,700 individual deductible, Contoso contributes $750 a year to the HSA.

## §4 Retirement
401(k) with a 100% match on the first 4% of pay, vesting immediately.

## §5 Employee Assistance Program
Free, confidential counseling (6 sessions per issue), legal and financial guidance, available 24/7 at 1-800-555-0199.
